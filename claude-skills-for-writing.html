What are Claude Skills?

Claude Skills are predefined sets of instructions, tools, and contexts that you can give to Claude. For writers, this means defining your brand's style guide, preferred vocabulary, and formatting rules so the AI writes exactly like you—or your target audience—every single time.

Why use Skills for Writing?

  • Eliminate "AI Voice": Ban cliché phrases (like "In today's fast-paced world") to ensure your content sounds authentic and human.
  • Brand Consistency: Lock in your specific tone, formatting rules, and vocabulary across all generated drafts.
  • SEO Optimization: Automatically integrate target keywords and generate meta descriptions without compromising readability.

Practical SKILL.md Example

Below is a powerful skill for content creation. It ensures Claude writes engaging, well-structured articles while strictly avoiding common AI writing habits.

# Skill: Blog Post & Content Editor ## Description Assists writers in drafting, editing, and optimizing blog posts, articles, and newsletters with a focus on readability, engagement, and SEO. ## Instructions 1. Analyze the target audience and desired tone (e.g., professional, conversational, witty). 2. Structure the content with clear, engaging H2 and H3 headings. 3. Ensure high readability: use short paragraphs, active voice, and varied sentence lengths. 4. If SEO keywords are provided, integrate them naturally without "keyword stuffing." 5. Always generate a compelling, 150-character meta description at the end. ## Constraints - NEVER use cliché AI phrases (e.g., "In today's fast-paced world", "Delve into", "Unlock the power of", "Tapestry"). - Maintain the author's original voice and style if editing existing text. - Do not use emojis unless explicitly requested. - Keep the introduction under 100 words and get straight to the point.

Installation Guide

Set up this writing skill in your workspace in three simple steps:

  1. Create a folder in your project root named .claude/skills/ (or keep it in your personal writing folder).
  2. Create a file named blog-editor.md and paste the code from the example above.
  3. When drafting or editing, simply say: "Use the Blog Post skill to rewrite this draft to be more conversational and engaging..."

Example Prompts

"Activate the Blog Post skill. Here is a rough outline for an article about 'Remote Work Productivity'. Please draft the introduction and first H2 section in a witty, conversational tone."

"Using the Content Editor skill, review this draft. Remove any cliché AI phrases, shorten the paragraphs, and ensure the target keyword 'AI writing tools' is included naturally."

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I stop the AI from sounding like a robot?

The "Constraints" section in the SKILL.md is your best friend here. By explicitly banning overused AI words (like "delve", "tapestry", or "unlock"), you force the AI to use more natural, human vocabulary.

Can this skill match my specific brand voice?

Absolutely. You can paste a few examples of your past writing into the skill instructions and tell Claude: "Analyze these examples and mimic this exact tone and sentence structure in all future outputs."

Is this useful for technical documentation?

Yes! You can easily modify the skill to prioritize clarity, brevity, and technical accuracy over "engaging" prose. Just change the instructions to focus on step-by-step logic and precise terminology.
